No. JuiceBox is built around logging after the fact. Plug in, leave it overnight, and add the session whenever suits you. If you would rather start it there and then, from 1.3 you can tap Start Charge & Finish Later on a new session and your charge appears on the Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, Apple Watch and CarPlay while it runs. Costs are calculated from the rates and figures you enter, so they are as accurate as your inputs. Predictions are estimates from your own charging patterns and get better the more sessions you log. Treat them as a guide rather than a guarantee.
Yes, from 1.3. Save your peak and off-peak rates in Settings under Electric Tariff and they turn into quick fill buttons when you log a session. Start a Pending Charge and JuiceBox works out when your charge actually starts and finishes from your off-peak hours, or from your ready-by time if you are on a smart EV tariff. If a home charge straddled both rates, turn on Split Tariff to divide the kWh between them and get the true cost.
Yes, from 1.3. Open a vehicle in Driveway and choose Running Costs to add your MOT, service, road tax and insurance dates. With Pro, the Expense Log also tracks services, repairs, tax, insurance and finance, recording your monthly payments so you can see what your EV really costs to run with charging included.

Each report carries its own reference and checks your readings and rates, so the numbers stand up to scrutiny.
